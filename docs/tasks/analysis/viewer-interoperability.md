# Viewer interoperability

## Goal

Check by hand that real viewers open and display each shipped destination as
intended: the manual half of
[`analysis/display-output-acceptance`](display-output-acceptance.md), split out on
2026-10-01. The Android check it absorbed from `output/gain-map-dialect-activation`
moved to [`analysis/android-gain-map-check`](android-gain-map-check.md) on 2026-10-04
(low priority; no device).

## Design

For each viewer, OS and display-HDR setting, record these binary observations per
file:

1. it opens without repair or error;
2. the viewer reports or visibly selects the intended SDR or HDR rendition;
3. turning HDR off, or an SDR-only reader, falls back as the destination specifies;
4. orientation, dimensions and crop are correct (no output carries extra channels);
5. no gross channel swap, inversion, all-black or all-white render, or edge artifact.

Readers:

- macOS (Preview, Photos), display HDR on and off;
- iPhone (Photos);
- Chrome on macOS, the non-Apple gain-map reader;
- one SDR-only reader, for the gain-map files.

"Plausible", "looks good" and agreement with a remembered scene are not pass
criteria. A failure becomes its own follow-up task, not an inline fix.

Decided 2026-10-04 (user):

- **A small fixed file set**: the synthetic chart fixture plus two real frames with
  real highlights, through every ready destination row and the film master, so the
  set can be re-checked after a container change. Generated by `nctool viewer set`
  (procedure in `scripts/viewer-interop/`), never committed.
- **Item 3 is pass/fail only for the gain-map JPEG**, whose SDR base is its specified
  fallback. No fallback is specified for the HDR TIFFs (linear, PQ, HLG) or the film
  master; for those item 3 records what the reader does.
- TIFFs go to the Apple readers only; Chrome and the SDR-only reader get the gain-map
  JPEGs.
- **libultrahdr's `ultrahdr_app` decodes the gain-map files as a pre-check**, beside
  `scripts/iso-decoder-oracle/`. It is the cheap proxy for Android's parser, not a
  substitute for the device.

## How to Verify

Every rubric item is recorded in `docs/progress/analysis.md` with the viewer, OS and
display setting and the evidence, for every reader above, and every failure has a
follow-up task.
